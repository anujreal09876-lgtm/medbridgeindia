Consultation Support

About MedBridge India

MedBridge India is a healthcare coordination and medical-travel assistance service for international patients seeking care in India. We work alongside your chosen providers, not in place of them.

Important: MedBridge India does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace qualified medical professionals. Treatment decisions, suitability, pricing, availability and outcomes are determined by the healthcare provider and patient, not by MedBridge India.

Privacy Policy

MedBridge India respects your privacy and is committed to handling your information responsibly.
